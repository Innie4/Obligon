This was a separate self-critique, not an independent critic. Website adaptation: iOS-native fluency is scored literally and remains below the iOS stop threshold. The release does not claim to satisfy App Store or top-studio design standards.

Round-one changes: retain product identity (landed); display publication evidence next to decisions (landed); separate success from error feedback (landed); remove fabricated metrics/references (landed); verify mobile fit (landed at 390×844). Round-two concrete defects—fuel name aliases and unknown prices rendered as zero—were corrected and subsequently retested. No further decorative redesign was introduced.

Stop result: **not done by the original iOS studio stop rule**. Work ends at the authorized responsive-web integration scope, with the honest scores above. Mechanical scans passed for all three studies; those checks do not establish usability or native quality.
